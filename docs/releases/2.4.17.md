# DeadRecall 2.4.17

DeadRecall 2.4.17 updates the bundled Villagers module so Miners must occupy a
real exposed work face before any mining progress or block mutation can occur.
The outer JAR remains a migration-only compatibility host.

## Miner Work-Face Correction

- A Miner no longer treats a broad four-block radius as a valid work
  location. It must stand horizontally adjacent to the selected block, with
  that block between foot and eye height.
- Standing directly above a buried target cannot advance work or produce
  materials.
- Navigation resolves an exposed adjacent stance through its supporting block,
  preventing vanilla pathfinding from choosing the top of the mining target.
- New Mine shelters keep their side gate open so vanilla Villager navigation
  can enter the spiral shaft. Existing generated Mines are recognized by their
  Furnace and first stair, then their old closed gate is reopened safely.
- A structure-spawned Villager whose airborne flag is stale is treated as
  grounded only when a sturdy block is physically supporting its feet.
- Bounded target scans no longer spend their 512-check budget on coordinates
  outside the assigned Work Zone, so reachable lower faces are not skipped
  while the Miner is approaching from outside.
- At the work face, the Miner stops navigating, faces the selected block, and
  only then advances the configured work duration and swings its tool.
- A second validation in the mining commit rejects any stale or incorrectly
  scheduled target unless the Miner is still at a legal work face.
