# DeadRecall 2.4.18

DeadRecall 2.4.18 updates the bundled Villagers module so a generated village
Mine becomes a real, progressively descending excavation instead of restoring
the same stone face forever. The outer JAR remains a migration-only
compatibility host.

## Progressive Generated Mines

- Every successful Mine source now remains destroyed after its exact live loot
  and pickaxe wear commit.
- A generated village Mine appends one deeper tread per successful work cycle,
  following the original covered 5×5 spiral with a cobblestone stair,
  three-block head clearance, roof, inner guard rail and a new outward natural
  work face.
- The Mine Work Zone's lower boundary is persisted only after the complete
  tread succeeds, so later target scans and world restarts continue downward.
- Fluids, containers, live entities, protected or unloaded cells, unsafe
  materials and the world floor stop extension without restoring the source or
  leaving a partial tread.
- Player-created Miner Zones remain finite and never gain automatic structure
  construction.
