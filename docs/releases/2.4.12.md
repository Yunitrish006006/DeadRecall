# DeadRecall 2.4.12

DeadRecall 2.4.12 updates Totem Villagers with reliable natural-village work,
a dedicated Lumberjack appearance, and a complete audited trade catalogue.

## Exact Versions

- TotemCore `0.6.0`
- TotemRemnant `0.2.11`
- TotemDiscordBridge `0.1.6`
- TotemAutomata `0.1.12`
- TotemAlchemy `0.1.23`
- TotemEnchanting `0.1.5`
- TotemExcavation `0.1.2`
- TotemLocksmith `0.1.0`
- TotemVanillaTweaks `0.1.8`
- TotemNexus `0.2.6`
- TotemVillagers `0.1.24`

## Natural Village Work

- Miner and Lumberjack target searches now examine nearby horizontal layers
  before spending their bounded scan budget at depth.
- Workers path to a reachable adjacent standing position instead of attempting
  to path into the solid block they need to mine or chop.
- Founding Fisherman and recruited Toolsmith professions reserve and retain
  their exact job-site POIs while the natural workforce is created.
- Two naturally generated Mangrove villages were exercised until both Miner
  and Lumberjack work produced real tool-durability changes.

## Appearance

- Lumberjacks now use a dedicated vanilla-layout 64×64 profession texture with
  a restrained forest-green cap and leather work apron.

## Trade Audit

- Review sheets are generated from the live trade registries and production
  policies for all thirteen vanilla and four Totem villager professions.
- The Toolsmith backpack-material trade now splits two non-stackable Bundles
  across both merchant input slots instead of creating an impossible stack.
- Trade snapshot client Mixins are registered so the physical work-inventory
  panel is included in client review and validation.
- Builder and Guard remain intentionally non-trading professions.

## Validation

- Totem Villagers passed all 120 required Fabric GameTests on Java 25.
- The complete client suite passed, including generated-village work,
  profession retention, dedicated skin rendering, and trade review sheets.
- The DeadRecall bundle is checked for eleven exact nested module IDs,
  versions, hashes, metadata dependencies, and duplicate-free loading.
